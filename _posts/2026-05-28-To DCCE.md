I sent you messages this and last year. You didn't reply on both times. That hurts, twice.

P asked me once why I am so scared of telling you when everyone else knows. 

What ran to my mind was: "Look at her. She's insanely out-of-this-world gorgeous. She's always the kindest person in the room. She's emotionally mature. She's such an effortless achiever. And she's rich enough to work for a government that pays crap, without worrying about the future. I'm just an average looking guy who gets triggered by stupid people on the road. And oh, I can't even achieve anything because I'm so unstable. She's way out of my league."

But P would just tell me that those reasons didn't stop me before.

I think I said something between the lines of not want something this wonderful to disappear. To have a constant positive in my life despite its ups and downs.

Looking back, there's a lot of truth to that. As a self-preservation, and wanting to keep you in my life, I'm good with being on the sidelines. 

I like you so much that we would not have worked out. 

I missed you. 

Happy birthday.
