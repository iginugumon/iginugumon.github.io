# I miss the beach more than you


I reached for my phone
Scrolled through my feed
Checked your story
Checked your post

I don’t really care that much
It’s just a habit I can’t shake

I sighed deeply
Drank some water 
Closed my eyes
Looked at the door

I’m not waiting 
I know it’ll open at two

I don’t like you 
Nothing’s wrong
I’m just a liar
And I miss the beach more than you
